| unknown | codex | gpt-6-luna | low | all four at 3/3; Luna cheapest |

**Read this before relying on it.** Codex now carries an estimated price, so the ranking no
longer rests on a stored zero, as the 2026-10-15 table did. But every row is a tie on pass rate,
broken by cost. At a 100% pass rate on six tasks with three repetitions, the run cannot tell Luna
at low effort from Sol or Sonnet on harder work. Luna's earlier silent failures (2026-10-14,
2026-10-15) did not recur here. They are the reason to keep watching this row on real runs.
No row exists for `review`, `refactor`, `plan` or `ops`: this suite has no task of those
purposes. `suites/routing/purposes.json` and `real-work.json` have them, and neither has run
yet.
